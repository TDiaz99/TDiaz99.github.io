Off-cycle payments

An off-cycle payment pays someone outside the normal schedule. Use it for corrections, final pay and urgent one-offs.

When to use one

  • A payroll error that cannot wait for the next run.
  • Final pay for a leaver who has already left.
  • A signing bonus agreed between pay dates.

An off-cycle payment is a real pay run. It files its own tax submission and produces its own payslip, so it appears separately in year-end totals.

Create an off-cycle payment

  1. Go to Payroll > Pay runs and select New off-cycle run.

  2. Choose the pay date. It must be at least two business days ahead.

  3. Add the employees to be paid. Only those with verified bank details appear.

  4. Enter the amounts against the relevant earnings codes.

  5. Choose how tax should be calculated. See the table below.

  6. Select Prepare, then have an approver release it.

Tax calculation options

Option What it does Use it for
Standard Treats the payment as if it were part of the normal period Corrections to normal pay
Supplemental Applies a flat rate to the whole payment Bonuses and commission
Final pay Includes accrued unused leave and closes the record Departing employees

Choosing Final pay marks the employee as terminated and stops all future accrual. It cannot be undone without a support request.

Costs

Off-cycle runs carry a per-run processing fee. The fee is shown before you approve, and appears on your next invoice.

If a correction can wait, adding it to the next scheduled run costs nothing and keeps year-end reporting simpler.
