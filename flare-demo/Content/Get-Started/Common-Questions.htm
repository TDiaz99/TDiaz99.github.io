Common questions

Short answers to the questions new administrators ask most often.

Setup

Can I run payroll before setup is finished?

No. Your company profile, one pay group and a verified bank account must all be in place first.

How many pay groups can I create?

As many as you need. Most companies use one per pay frequency.

Employees can only belong to one pay group at a time.

Can I change a pay frequency later?

Only before the group has a completed pay run. After that, create a new pay group and move the employees across.

Employees

Can employees update their own details?

Yes. Send an onboarding invitation and they can enter tax and bank details themselves.

Changes to a pay rate always require an administrator.

What happens if I enter the wrong start date?

Edit it on the employee record. Leave balances recalculate overnight.

If a pay run has already used the old date, contact support so the period can be corrected.

Can I import employees in bulk?

Yes. Go to People > Import and download the template. Meridian validates every row before anything is saved.

Billing

How is Meridian priced?

Per active employee, per month. Employees who leave part way through a month are billed for that month only.

Still stuck? Email support@meridian.example and include your company name.
